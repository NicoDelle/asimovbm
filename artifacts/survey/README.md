# Survey artifact provenance

Six older clips have been recovered from Git history and can be viewed in the
[archived video gallery](../historical-videos/index.html). Their bytes are
unchanged from commit `703e72b4c4fe644456ee7f4c7b8795fdee660295`, immediately
before their removal in `d3f24b22f1d6375dce0df004d733c41e1b00ef1a`.
The recovery manifest records original paths, Git blob IDs, SHA-256 hashes and
durations. They cover only the historical `policy_a` path and two views.

The exact set of four videos rated by the 120 participants has not been
identified. The recovered clips' correspondence to those stimuli or the
twelve curated real-policy traces cannot be verified from the remaining
metadata. This gap does not establish that different stimuli were used.

`json/policy_a/` contains six preserved legacy sidecars, covering three
scenarios in `arrival` and `bystander` views. Their metadata identifies
kinematic rendering and older source episode IDs. They are historical export
artifacts, **not verified provenance for the study's four rated videos**.
Their video paths do not imply those files are present. Do not use them to
infer the policy weights, trajectories, or stimuli shown to participants.

The six recovered clips live under `artifacts/historical-videos/`, rather than
the active survey application's discovery directory. To recover a clip again,
use `git show 703e72b4c4fe644456ee7f4c7b8795fdee660295:<original_path>` and verify
the bytes against that gallery's manifest. For example, from the repo root:

```bash
git show 703e72b4c4fe644456ee7f4c7b8795fdee660295:artifacts/survey/videos/policy_a/arrival/g1_point_to_point_open.mp4 > /tmp/asimovbm-historical-open.mp4
```

For inspectable motion, use the
[telemetry replay gallery](../reconstructed-videos/index.html), generated by
`tools/replay_paper_traces.py` from the twelve released real-policy traces.
It replays recorded full robot poses and obstacle positions with the robot
model, without rerunning policy inference or dynamics. It includes individual
clips, four compilations and a hash manifest. These are new reconstructed
videos, not recovered originals. See
[reproduction instructions](../../paper/README.md#view-or-regenerate-episode-videos)
and [implementation notes](../../paper/IMPLEMENTATION_NOTES.md).

If originals are recovered later, retain their unmodified bytes, publish their
SHA-256 hashes and source/permission information, and explicitly map each
rated video to policy, viewpoint, scenario segments and trace IDs. Keep these
reconstructions separately identified.
